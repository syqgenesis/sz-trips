## Decisions needed

1. **Founding price: $8,900 or $9,900?** The countdown block says "first five approved founders to book get founding pricing at **$9,900**". The price ladder and the application question both say seats 1–5 are **$8,900** ($9,900 is the Early tier, seats 6–12). The copy below uses $8,900.
2. **Deposit or full payment at booking?** "How it works" says the payment link drops on 9 Nov and seats are confirmed on payment. But "How booking works" says "Balance due 14 December" and "refund every deposit", and the FAQ says the final host list arrives "before the balance is due". That implies a deposit at booking and the balance on 14 Dec, but **no deposit amount is given anywhere**. The "When do I pay?" answer below assumes a deposit model, with the amount left out.
3. **Trip confirmation: 30 Nov or 1 Dec?** The booking steps and trust line say we confirm on **30 November**. The approval email says "hold off on flights until we confirm the trip on **1 December**". Pick one date and use it everywhere (or say: 10 bookings needed by 30 Nov, confirmation email on 1 Dec).
4. **The $500 same-company discount is in the doc.** It's the last line of "Cohort and Price": "Two people from the same company each get $500 off." Keep it or cut it. There is also a second discount that only appears in the interview questions: "Each accepted referral gets you $500 off." It isn't on the pricing section. Should it be, and do the two stack?
5. **BrainCo is based in Hangzhou, not Shenzhen.** Every source puts its HQ in Hangzhou (about 2 hours by plane). Check that Friday's visit is at a Shenzhen site, or move BrainCo to "also under consideration".
6. **Small wording point:** "first five approved *founders*" also excludes investors and operators, who can apply too. The copy below says "applicants".

### FAQ: the three answers that need to agree

**Why do I have to apply?**
We keep the group to 25–30 people and pick a mix of founders, investors and operators who'll get value from each other. Your application also tells us what you're building, so we can match visits and supplier meetings to it. Applying is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

**What's the interview?**
A short call after you apply, to check the trip suits you. We'll ask what you're building, what your biggest manufacturing or sourcing problem is, what you want to leave Shenzhen with, and whether the dates and price work for you. It isn't a technical test. If you're approved, you'll get your booking link when seats open on 9 November.

**When do I pay?**
Not when you apply. Seats open to approved applicants on 9 November at 9am PT. You pay a deposit when you book, which confirms your seat, and the balance is due 14 December. Seats go in the order people book, and the earliest bookings get the lowest prices. If fewer than 10 people have booked by 30 November, the trip doesn't run and you get a full refund.
